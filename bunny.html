怎么玩How to play

点「开始聆听」，允许麦克风，然后放音乐。低音蹦出又胖又大的兔子，高音蹦出小小圆圆的兔子，最多同时 10 只，满了就挤掉最老的那只。 Hit Listen, allow the mic, then play music. Low notes make big chubby bunnies, high notes make tiny round ones. Ten at a time — the oldest gets bumped.

不用麦克风也行No mic needed

按 1–5 手动放一只对应音区的兔子，或者直接点画面。 Press 1–5 to drop a bunny from that band by hand, or just click the stage.

听不到声音？Hearing nothing?

用外放而不是耳机，音量开大一点。代码里已经关掉了 echoCancellation，否则浏览器会把你自己音箱的声音当回声消掉。 Use speakers, not headphones, and turn it up. echoCancellation is off in the code — otherwise the browser cancels your own speakers as echo.

关于「音高」On pitch

背景音乐是复音的，真正的 F0 检测不可靠，所以这里用的是加权频谱峰值（带 +6 dB/oct tilt 抵消音乐的低频滚降），实际反映的是主导音区。 Music is polyphonic, so true F0 detection is unreliable. This reads the weighted spectral peak with a +6 dB/oct tilt — it tracks the dominant register rather than a single fundamental.

用麦克风听环境里的音乐，按主导音区分成五档：音越低，蹦出来的兔子越胖越大；音越高，越小越圆。同时最多十只。

Listens through your mic and sorts what it hears into five registers — low notes make big chubby bunnies, high notes make small round ones. Ten on stage at a time.
