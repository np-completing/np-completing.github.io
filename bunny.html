怎么玩How to play

点「开始聆听」，允许麦克风，然后放音乐或弄出点声响。低音蹦出又胖又大的兔子，高音蹦出小小圆圆的兔子，最多同时 10 只，满了就挤掉最老的那只。 Hit Listen, allow the mic, then play music or just make some noise. Low notes make big chubby bunnies, high notes make tiny round ones. Ten at a time — the oldest gets bumped.

不用麦克风也行No mic needed

放歌：点顶栏的「放歌」选一个本地音频文件，声音直接送进分析，完全不经过麦克风——不用授权、不受环境噪声干扰、信号也干净得多。手机上尤其推荐。
手动：按 1–7 放一只对应音区的兔子。手机上点左边音高梯的任意一格，效果一样。 File — pick a local audio file and it goes straight into the analyser: no microphone, no permission prompt, no room noise, and a much cleaner signal. On a phone this is by far the most reliable route.
By hand — press 1–7 to drop a bunny from that band. On a phone, tap any rung of the pitch ladder instead.

听不到声音？Hearing nothing?

先看状态栏那条粉色电平条：一直是空的说明麦克风没数据；有跳动但兔子不出来，就把「灵敏」往右拉。用外放而不是耳机，音量开大一点。 Watch the pink level bar in the status strip. Flat means no signal at all; moving but no bunnies means you should raise Sens. Use speakers, not headphones.

iPhone 上收不到On iPhone

iOS Safari 会无视 echoCancellation: false，把回声消除和降噪焊死在采集链路里，而且开麦后输出会从扬声器改路由到听筒。所以同一台 iPhone 外放的音乐会被当成自己的回声消掉——状态栏出现橙色「系统强制回声消除」就是这个情况。最可靠的办法是点顶栏的「放歌」直接喂一个音频文件，完全不走麦克风。或者用另一台设备放音乐，iPhone 只负责听。另外一定要用 Safari 本体打开，微信等 App 内置浏览器通常不给麦克风权限。 iOS Safari ignores echoCancellation: false — the voice-processing unit is baked into the capture path — and routes output to the earpiece once the mic opens. Music played from the same iPhone gets cancelled as its own echo. The reliable fix is the File button, which feeds an audio file straight in without touching the microphone. Failing that, play the music from a second device. Open in Safari itself; in-app browsers usually block the mic.

踩着拍子落地Landing on the beat

从起音包络估计 BPM，七档按 8:4:2 的嵌套倍数分配：低音档八拍落一次、高音档两拍一次，于是每两小节全体同时落地一次。地面线随拍脉动，全体落地时泛起紫色底光；状态栏的八个圆点是节拍位置，左边的指示灯亮起表示已锁定。节奏太散的音乐锁不上，兔子会退回自由弹跳。 BPM is estimated from the onset envelope; the seven bands bounce every 8, 4 or 2 beats, so everyone lands together once every two bars. The eight dots in the status strip show the beat position. Music without a clear pulse won't lock, and the bunnies fall back to free bouncing.

一些彩蛋Easter eggs

八度纠缠：当两个音正好差一个八度、而且强度接近时，会蹦出一对镜像兔子——它们关于画面中心严格反相关，中间连着一根虚线，身上各套一圈光环。点其中任意一只，链接就断开，两只从此各走各的。
午夜：本地时间 23 点到次日 4 点之间打开会变成星空。其他时候想看的话，敲 night 五个字母，或者长按左上角的标题不放，都能切换。 Octave entanglement — when two pitches an octave apart arrive at similar strength, a mirrored pair appears, anticorrelated through the centre of the stage and joined by a dashed line. Click either one to cut it.
Midnight — open the page between 23:00 and 04:00 local time and the sky comes out. Any other time, type night or long-press the wordmark to toggle it.

关于「音高」On pitch

背景音乐是复音的，真正的 F0 检测不可靠，所以这里用的是加权频谱峰值（带 +6 dB/oct tilt 抵消音乐的低频滚降），实际反映的是主导音区。 Music is polyphonic, so true F0 detection is unreliable. This reads the weighted spectral peak with a +6 dB/oct tilt — it tracks the dominant register rather than a single fundamental.

用麦克风听环境里的音乐，按主导音区分成七档：音越低，蹦出来的兔子越胖越大；音越高，越小越圆。同时最多十只，落地时刻踩着音乐的拍子。

Listens through your mic and sorts what it hears into seven registers — low notes make big chubby bunnies, high notes make small round ones. Ten on stage at a time.
